Using Balances

Author
Affiliation

Marc Los Huertos

EA Program, Pomona College

Published

2026-05-27

1. Purpose

This procedure describes the proper use, zeroing, taring, and routine quality checks for the laboratory balances in the EA Program. Correct weighing technique is fundamental to virtually every quantitative laboratory procedure. For calibration and verification protocols, see SOP-L200 Analytical Balance Calibration.

2. Scope

  • Applies to: All Mettler Toledo balances in the EA Program laboratory, including the basic balances (MS1602TS/00) and the precision balance (XPE205 DeltaRange)
  • Does not apply to: Portable field balances or calibration procedures (see SOP-L200)
  • Location: EA Program laboratory, SGM 133

3. Responsibilities

Role Responsibility
Lab PI Approve SOP, ensure training compliance
Trained Personnel Follow this SOP, report malfunctions
New Students Complete training before independent use

4. Safety and Hazards

WarningHazards
  • Physical: Dropping heavy items onto the balance pan can damage the weighing cell. Handle objects carefully and never exceed the rated capacity.
  • Chemical: Some materials weighed on the balance may be hazardous. Refer to the relevant SOP for the material being weighed. Never place chemicals directly on the weighing pan; always use a sample vessel.

Required PPE

Emergency Procedures

If a chemical spill occurs on or near the balance, clean the spill per SOP-L003 Hazardous Materials Handling before resuming use.

6. Procedure

6.1 Switching On and Warm-Up

  1. The balance switches on automatically when connected to the power supply
  2. Before the balance gives reliable results, it must acclimatize to room temperature and warm up while connected to power. Refer to the instrument manual for the recommended warm-up time for each model.
  3. To exit standby mode, press the power/standby key. The display turns on and the balance is ready immediately.
Note

If the balance was completely disconnected from power for an extended period, allow it to warm up for at least 30 minutes before use (Harris & Lucy, 2020).

6.2 Zeroing the Balance

  1. Open the draft shield (precision balance only)
  2. Ensure the weighing pan is clear
  3. Close the draft shield
  4. Press the Zero key. The display reads 0.0000 g (precision) or 0.0 g (basic)

6.3 Taring

If a sample vessel is used, tare the balance before adding sample material:

  1. Zero the balance (Section 6.2)
  2. Open the draft shield
  3. Place the sample vessel on the center of the weighing pan
  4. Close the draft shield
  5. Press the Tare key. The display reads zero and the Net indicator appears

6.4 Performing a Weighing

  1. Open the draft shield (if applicable)
  2. Place the weighing object into the tared sample vessel
  3. Close the draft shield and wait for the stability indicator
  4. Record the displayed mass in the lab notebook
  5. If using the XPE205 electronic results feature, tap Add result to append the value to the results list

6.5 Completing and Saving Results (XPE205)

  1. Tap Complete to open the task completion dialog
  2. Select an option to save or print the results list
  3. Follow the on-screen instructions
  4. Tap Complete to finalize; the results list is saved or printed and then cleared

6.6 Shutdown

  1. Remove all samples and vessels from the weighing pan
  2. Clean the pan and draft shield interior with a lint-free wipe
  3. Close the draft shield
  4. To enter standby mode, hold the power/standby key. The display goes dark but the balance remains powered on and thermally stable.
  5. To switch the balance off completely, disconnect from the power supply. Note that the balance will require a full warm-up before its next use.

7. Quality Control and Acceptance Criteria

7.1 Leveling the Balance

Exact horizontal positioning is essential for repeatable and accurate results:

  1. Check the spirit level before each use session
  2. If the message “Balance is out of level” appears, tap Level the balance to open the leveling aid
  3. Adjust the leveling feet following the on-screen instructions until the bubble is centered
  4. The leveling aid can also be accessed via Balance menu > Leveling aid

7.2 Internal Adjustment (XPE205)

  1. Navigate to Methods > Adjustments
  2. Verify that the adjustment strategy is set to Internal adjustment
  3. Open the Methods section, tap Adjustments, select the adjustment, and tap Start (or from the main weighing screen, tap More > Start adjustment)
  4. Do not touch the balance during the adjustment
  5. When complete, review the adjustment results
  6. Tap Print if a record is needed, then tap Finish adjustment

For external calibration verification using traceable reference weights, see SOP-L200.

8. Waste Disposal

No waste is generated by this procedure. Cleaning materials (Kimwipes) can be disposed of in standard trash unless they contacted hazardous chemicals, in which case follow SOP-L003.

10. Training Record

Before using the laboratory balances independently, personnel must:

  1. Read this SOP in full
  2. Complete SOP-L002 Laboratory Safety training
  3. Demonstrate proper leveling, zeroing, taring, and weighing technique under supervision
  4. For the XPE205 precision balance, also demonstrate use of the draft shield and internal adjustment procedure
  5. Be signed off by the PI or lab manager
