\section{Purpose}\label{purpose}

This SOP describes the general operation, calibration, maintenance, and
shutdown of the Shimadzu TOC-L total organic carbon analyzer and its
companion modules: the TNM-L Total Nitrogen Module and the SSM-5000A
Solid Sample Module. The TOC-L uses a 680 C combustion catalytic
oxidation method, which is one of two principal approaches to TOC
analysis (the other being wet chemical oxidation with persulfate); the
high-temperature combustion method is preferred for environmental
samples because it more completely oxidizes refractory organic matter
such as humic substances (Sharp, 1993; Sugimura \& Suzuki, 1988).

All instrument control is performed through the LabSolutions TOC
software on the dedicated computer.

This SOP covers the instrument itself. For application-specific sample
preparation, measurement modes, and calculations, refer to the relevant
analytical SOPs:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \href{SOP-L311-dissolved-organic-carbon-water.qmd}{SOP-L311 Dissolved
  Organic Carbon in Water} (NPOC)
\item
  \href{SOP-L407-total-carbon.qmd}{SOP-L407 Total Carbon (Soils)} (SSM)
\item
  \href{SOP-L409-microbial-biomass-carbon.qmd}{SOP-L409 Microbial
  Biomass Carbon} (CFE extracts)
\item
  \href{SOP-L418-water-extractable-doc.qmd}{SOP-L418 Water-Extractable
  DOC} (soil extracts)
\end{itemize}

\section{Definitions}\label{definitions}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Catalytic combustion:} Oxidation of organic carbon in the
  sample at 680 °C over a platinum catalyst in the presence of oxygen;
  the standard method used by the Shimadzu TOC-L for liquid samples.
\item
  \textbf{Dissolved organic carbon (DOC):} Organic carbon in a water or
  soil extract sample that passes through a 0.45 µm filter; the NPOC
  method is the standard approach for DOC measurement.
\item
  \textbf{IC (inorganic carbon):} Carbon present as carbonate,
  bicarbonate, and dissolved CO\textsubscript{2}; removed by
  acidification and sparging before NPOC measurement.
\item
  \textbf{NDIR detector (non-dispersive infrared detector):} A detector
  that measures CO\textsubscript{2} concentration by its absorption of
  infrared light at \textasciitilde4.3 µm; the detection element in the
  Shimadzu TOC-L.
\item
  \textbf{NPOC (non-purgeable organic carbon):} The organic carbon
  fraction remaining in a sample after acidification and sparging to
  remove inorganic carbon and purgeable volatile organic carbon; the
  operationally defined DOC measured by the Shimadzu TOC-L.
\item
  \textbf{SSM-5000A:} The Shimadzu Solid Sample Module that combusts
  dried, ground solid materials (soils, sediments, plant tissue)
  directly in ceramic boats at up to 900 °C for total carbon analysis.
\item
  \textbf{TC (total carbon):} The sum of all carbon forms (organic and
  inorganic) in a sample; measured by direct combustion without
  acidification.
\item
  \textbf{TOC (total organic carbon):} TC minus IC; in practice, usually
  determined as NPOC for environmental samples.
\item
  \textbf{TON (total organic nitrogen):} Total nitrogen minus inorganic
  nitrogen; measured simultaneously by the TNM-L module using
  chemiluminescence detection of NO produced during combustion.
\end{itemize}

\section{Safety and Hazards}\label{safety-and-hazards}

\begin{tcolorbox}[enhanced jigsaw, arc=.35mm, bottomrule=.15mm, bottomtitle=1mm, breakable, colback=white, colbacktitle=quarto-callout-warning-color!10!white, colframe=quarto-callout-warning-color-frame, coltitle=black, left=2mm, leftrule=.75mm, opacityback=0, opacitybacktitle=0.6, rightrule=.15mm, title=\textcolor{quarto-callout-warning-color}{\faExclamationTriangle}\hspace{0.5em}{Hazards}, titlerule=0mm, toprule=.15mm, toptitle=1mm]

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Thermal:} The combustion furnace operates at 680 C in liquid
  analysis mode and up to 900 C in SSM mode. Ceramic boats, combustion
  tubes, and furnace surfaces can cause severe burns.
\item
  \textbf{Chemical:} Hydrochloric acid (2 M HCl) is used for
  acidification and inorganic carbon sparging. Phosphoric acid (25\%) is
  used as the IC reagent. Avoid skin and eye contact with both.
\item
  \textbf{Compressed gas:} The instrument requires high-purity carrier
  gas (air or oxygen) supplied from a pressurized cylinder at
  approximately 200 kPa. Follow all compressed gas safety procedures
  (National Research Council, 2011).
\item
  \textbf{Electrical:} The instrument operates on high-voltage mains
  power. Do not open instrument panels or attempt internal repairs.
\item
  \textbf{Ozone:} The TNM-L chemiluminescence detector uses an ozone
  generator. The instrument vents ozone through an internal decomposer,
  but ensure that the exhaust line is connected and unobstructed. If an
  ozone odor is detected, shut down the TNM-L and ventilate the area.
\end{itemize}

\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{Emergency Procedures}\label{emergency-procedures}

For acid splashes, flush the affected area with water for at least 15
minutes. For burns from hot surfaces, apply cool running water and seek
first aid. If ozone is detected, shut down the TNM-L, open windows, and
leave the room until the odor dissipates. Refer to
\href{SOP-L002-laboratory-safety.qmd}{SOP-L002 Laboratory Safety} and
\href{SOP-L003-hazardous-materials-handling.qmd}{SOP-L003 Hazardous
Materials Handling}.

\section{Instrument Description}\label{instrument-description}

\subsection{System Overview}\label{system-overview}

The Shimadzu TOC-L is a combustion catalytic oxidation analyzer that
quantifies carbon (and, with the TNM-L, nitrogen) in liquid samples
(Shimadzu Corporation, 2019b). The system in the EA Program laboratory
consists of four main components:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{TOC-L analyzer:} Combusts liquid samples at 680 C over a
  platinum catalyst. The resulting CO\textsubscript{2} is measured by a
  non-dispersive infrared (NDIR) detector. The analyzer supports
  multiple measurement modes (TC, IC, TOC, NPOC, POC).
\item
  \textbf{TNM-L Total Nitrogen Module:} Measures total nitrogen (TN)
  simultaneously with carbon analysis. Nitrogen compounds in the
  combustion gas are converted to nitric oxide (NO), which is detected
  by a chemiluminescence detector after reaction with ozone (Shimadzu
  Corporation, 2019a). The TNM-L operates in parallel with the NDIR
  carbon detector, so carbon and nitrogen data are acquired from the
  same injection.
\item
  \textbf{SSM-5000A Solid Sample Module:} Accepts solid samples in
  ceramic boats for direct combustion at up to 900 C. Connects to the
  TOC-L for CO\textsubscript{2} detection. The TNM-L is not used during
  SSM operation.
\item
  \textbf{ASI-L Autosampler:} Holds up to 93 sample vials (8 mL or 40
  mL) for unattended sequential analysis of liquid samples. Includes an
  automated rinse function between injections.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Detection Principles}\label{detection-principles}

The TOC-L and TNM-L rely on two detectors operating in series on the
same combustion gas stream:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{NDIR detector (carbon):} Infrared absorption by
  CO\textsubscript{2} is proportional to the mass of carbon injected.
  The detector is selective for CO\textsubscript{2} and insensitive to
  other combustion gases.
\item
  \textbf{Chemiluminescence detector (nitrogen, TNM-L only):} NO in the
  combustion gas reacts with ozone (O\textsubscript{3}) to produce
  excited NO\textsubscript{2}*, which emits light as it returns to the
  ground state. The emitted light intensity is proportional to the mass
  of nitrogen injected (Walsh, 1989).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Instrument Startup (Cold
Start)}\label{instrument-startup-cold-start}

A cold start applies when the instrument has been fully powered down
(furnace off, gas closed). Allow approximately 60 minutes from power-on
to running samples.

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Open the carrier gas cylinder valve. Verify the regulator output
  pressure is set to 200 kPa at the instrument inlet. Check for leaks at
  all fittings (listen for hissing; apply leak-detection solution if
  uncertain).
\item
  Verify that the exhaust line from the TNM-L ozone decomposer is
  connected and routed to the fume hood or building exhaust
\item
  Check consumable levels:

  \begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
    IC reagent bottle (25\% phosphoric acid): refill if below the
    minimum line
  \item
    Dehumidifier / moisture trap: drain if water is visible
  \item
    Rinse water reservoir on the ASI-L: fill with fresh deionized water
  \end{itemize}
\item
  Power on the TOC-L main unit using the switch on the right side panel
\item
  If the TNM-L is needed, power on the TNM-L using the switch on its
  rear panel
\item
  Power on the ASI-L autosampler
\item
  Turn on the dedicated computer and launch LabSolutions TOC
\item
  In LabSolutions TOC, verify that communication with all modules is
  established. The status bar should show ``Warming Up''
\item
  \textbf{Wait for the furnace} to reach 680 C (approximately 20--30
  minutes). The software status changes from ``Warming Up'' to ``Ready''
\item
  \textbf{Wait for the TNM-L} ozone generator and chemiluminescence
  detector to stabilize (approximately 30--45 minutes after power-on;
  the TNM-L status indicator changes to ``Ready'')
\item
  Run 3--5 injections of deionized water as system conditioning blanks
  to flush the injection port, syringe, and combustion tube. Discard
  these results.
\item
  Run 3 additional deionized water blanks and verify baseline stability:
  TC blank values should be below 0.1 mg/L C; TN blank values should be
  below 0.1 mg/L N
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Instrument Startup (From
Standby/Sleep)}\label{instrument-startup-from-standbysleep}

When the instrument is in standby mode (furnace at temperature, gas
flowing, software idle), startup is faster:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Launch LabSolutions TOC if not already open
\item
  Verify that the status bar shows ``Ready'' for the TOC-L and (if
  applicable) the TNM-L
\item
  Check the IC reagent level and drain the moisture trap
\item
  Fill the ASI-L rinse reservoir with fresh deionized water
\item
  Run 3 deionized water conditioning blanks. If blanks are below 0.1
  mg/L C and 0.1 mg/L N, proceed to calibration.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Entering Active Standby}\label{entering-active-standby}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  After the final sample, run 3--5 deionized water blanks to flush the
  system
\item
  Leave the furnace at 680 C and the carrier gas flowing
\item
  Leave the TNM-L powered on
\item
  Empty the autosampler rack and cover with a dust cover
\item
  The instrument manager may set the software to a low carrier gas flow
  rate overnight to conserve gas (consult the manual for the minimum
  flow that maintains a positive purge of the combustion tube)
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Entering Reduced Standby}\label{entering-reduced-standby}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Flush the system with deionized water blanks as above
\item
  Power off the TNM-L (rear panel switch). This extends the ozone
  decomposer lifetime.
\item
  Leave the TOC-L furnace at 680 C and gas flowing
\item
  Cover the autosampler
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Entering Sleep Mode}\label{entering-sleep-mode}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Flush the system with deionized water blanks
\item
  Power off the TNM-L
\item
  In LabSolutions TOC, initiate a furnace cooldown (or simply power off
  the TOC-L)
\item
  Once the furnace temperature drops below 200 C (approximately 90
  minutes), close the carrier gas cylinder valve
\item
  Power off the ASI-L
\item
  Close LabSolutions TOC and shut down the computer
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Full Shutdown}\label{full-shutdown}

Follow the Sleep Mode steps, then:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Allow the furnace to cool to room temperature
\item
  Disconnect the carrier gas line if the instrument will be moved or
  serviced
\item
  Empty the IC reagent bottle if the shutdown will exceed 30 days
  (phosphoric acid can corrode the reagent lines over long idle periods)
\item
  Empty the ASI-L rinse reservoir to prevent microbial growth
\item
  Cover the instrument with a dust cover
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Software Navigation}\label{software-navigation}

LabSolutions TOC organizes work into the following areas:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Instrument Monitor:} Real-time display of furnace temperature,
  carrier gas flow, detector signal (NDIR and chemiluminescence), and
  system status
\item
  \textbf{Method Editor:} Define measurement mode (TC, IC, NPOC, TN,
  etc.), injection volume, number of replicate injections, sparge time
  (for NPOC), acidification volume, and calibration curve parameters
\item
  \textbf{Sample Table:} Enter sample IDs, vial positions, dilution
  factors, and measurement method for each sample
\item
  \textbf{Calibration:} Build, view, and edit calibration curves. The
  software fits curves by least-squares regression and reports
  r\textsuperscript{2} and residuals.
\item
  \textbf{Data Browser:} Review completed runs, view peak integrations,
  export results to CSV, and reprocess data
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Calibration}\label{calibration}

Calibrate at the start of each analytical session or whenever the
method, measurement mode, or concentration range changes. Carbon and
nitrogen calibrations are built independently but can be run
simultaneously if both detectors are active.

\subsubsection{Carbon Calibration}\label{carbon-calibration}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Prepare a multi-point calibration series from KHP dissolved in
  deionized water. Choose concentrations that bracket the expected
  sample range (e.g., 0, 1, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100 mg/L C). Prepare fresh
  daily or from a refrigerated stock per
  \href{SOP-L020-reagent-standard-preparation.qmd}{SOP-L020} (APHA et
  al., 2012).
\item
  For IC calibration, prepare standards from sodium bicarbonate/sodium
  carbonate in deionized water
\item
  In LabSolutions TOC, open the Method Editor and select the measurement
  mode (e.g., NPOC, TC, or TC-IC)
\item
  Enter calibration standard concentrations in the Calibration window
\item
  Load standards into the autosampler in order of increasing
  concentration
\item
  Run the calibration sequence. The software will perform replicate
  injections (typically 3) of each standard and fit a regression
\item
  Verify that r\textsuperscript{2} \(\geq\) 0.999 and that each standard
  back- calculates to within 10\% of its nominal value (15\% at the
  lowest standard is acceptable)
\item
  Save the calibration curve
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Nitrogen Calibration
(TNM-L)}\label{nitrogen-calibration-tnm-l}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Prepare a multi-point calibration series from KNO\textsubscript{3}
  dissolved in deionized water (e.g., 0, 0.5, 1, 5, 10, 25, 50 mg/L N)
\item
  In the Method Editor, confirm that TN measurement is enabled alongside
  the carbon mode
\item
  Enter TN calibration concentrations in the Calibration window
\item
  The TN calibration runs simultaneously with the carbon calibration if
  both are loaded in the same sample table; otherwise, run the TN
  standards as a separate sequence
\item
  Verify that r\textsuperscript{2} \(\geq\) 0.999 for the nitrogen curve
\item
  Save the calibration curve
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Setting Up an Analytical Run (Liquid
Samples)}\label{setting-up-an-analytical-run-liquid-samples}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  In the Method Editor, confirm the measurement mode, injection volume,
  number of replicate injections, and (for NPOC mode) sparge time and
  acid volume
\item
  In the Sample Table, enter the run sequence:

  \begin{enumerate}
  \def\labelenumii{\alph{enumii}.}
  \tightlist
  \item
    Calibration verification standard (mid-range) as the first sample
  \item
    Instrument blanks (deionized water) at the beginning, every 10--15
    samples, and the end of the run
  \item
    Method blanks appropriate to the application (e.g., 0.5 M
    K\textsubscript{2}SO\textsubscript{4} for soil extracts; consult the
    analytical method SOP)
  \item
    Sample vials in the order they are loaded in the autosampler
  \item
    A final calibration verification standard
  \end{enumerate}
\item
  Set the dilution factor for each sample if applicable
\item
  Verify that autosampler vial positions in the Sample Table match the
  physical rack positions
\item
  Confirm that the rinse reservoir on the autosampler is filled with
  deionized water
\item
  Click \textbf{Start} to begin the sequence
\end{enumerate}

The software default is 3 replicate injections per sample. If the
coefficient of variation (CV) among replicates exceeds 2\%, the software
can be configured to add up to 2 additional injections automatically
(set in Method Editor under ``Maximum Injections''). The outlier
replicate is excluded from the reported mean.

\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{Setting Up a Solid Sample Run
(SSM-5000A)}\label{setting-up-a-solid-sample-run-ssm-5000a}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  In LabSolutions TOC, switch to SSM mode
\item
  Pre-combust ceramic boats at 900 C in a muffle furnace
  (\href{SOP-L111-muffle-furnace.qmd}{SOP-L111}) to remove residual
  carbon. Allow boats to cool in a desiccator.
\item
  Weigh the solid sample into a clean ceramic boat. Record the mass to
  0.01 mg.
\item
  In the Sample Table, enter the sample ID, boat number, and sample mass
\item
  Place the ceramic boat on the SSM sample port and push it into the
  furnace when prompted by the software
\item
  The SSM combusts the sample; evolved CO\textsubscript{2} flows to the
  TOC-L NDIR detector
\item
  The software reports carbon content in mg C or as a percentage of
  sample mass
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Monitoring a Run in
Progress}\label{monitoring-a-run-in-progress}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Use the Instrument Monitor to watch real-time peak shapes and detector
  baselines
\item
  If a peak appears saturated (flat top), the sample exceeds the
  calibration range. Pause the run, dilute the sample, and re-queue.
\item
  If the CV among replicate injections exceeds 2\%, investigate (air
  bubble, particulates, insufficient sample volume) and consider
  re-running.
\item
  If the system blank rises above 0.5 mg/L C or 0.5 mg/L N during a run,
  the combustion tube or catalyst may need replacement. Complete the
  current run, note affected samples, and consult Section 9.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Quality Control}\label{quality-control}

QC procedures are tailored to each analytical method and are specified
in the relevant method SOP. The following instrument-level QC applies to
all runs (US Environmental Protection Agency, 2009):

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  System blanks (deionized water) at the start of every session must
  read below 0.1 mg/L C and 0.1 mg/L N
\item
  A calibration verification standard (mid-range) must be run at the
  beginning and end of every sample sequence, with recovery within
  90--110\%
\item
  Replicate injection CV must be below 2\% for liquid samples
\item
  All QC results are recorded in the instrument logbook and in the
  exported data file
\item
  For additional QC requirements (method blanks, duplicates, matrix
  spikes, continuing calibration verification frequency), refer to
  \href{SOP-L802-quality-control.qmd}{SOP-L802 Quality Control}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Catalyst Replacement
Procedure}\label{catalyst-replacement-procedure}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Allow the furnace to cool to room temperature (power off the TOC-L and
  wait at least 3 hours, or use the forced-air cooling function if
  available)
\item
  Wearing heat-resistant gloves and safety glasses, remove the
  combustion tube from the furnace per the manufacturer's procedure
\item
  Dispose of the spent catalyst (Pt on alumina) as hazardous waste
  (precious metal content; collect for EH\&S pickup per
  \href{SOP-L006-waste-disposal.qmd}{SOP-L006})
\item
  Fill the combustion tube with fresh catalyst to the manufacturer's
  specified depth (approximately 12 cm)
\item
  Add a thin layer of quartz wool above and below the catalyst bed
\item
  Reinstall the combustion tube. Reconnect all tubing and fittings.
\item
  Restart the furnace and allow it to reach 680 C
\item
  Run 20--30 deionized water conditioning injections to stabilize the
  new catalyst. Blanks should decrease progressively to below 0.1 mg/L
  C.
\item
  Run a calibration verification standard to confirm sensitivity before
  resuming sample analysis
\item
  Record the replacement date, catalyst lot number, and conditioning
  results in the instrument logbook
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Combustion Tube Inspection and
Replacement}\label{combustion-tube-inspection-and-replacement}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Remove the combustion tube as described in Section 9.2, step 2
\item
  Inspect for cracks, discoloration, or accumulation of non-volatile
  residue on the inner walls
\item
  If the tube is damaged, replace with a new quartz combustion tube
\item
  Repack with catalyst and condition as in Section 9.2
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Syringe and Needle
Maintenance}\label{syringe-and-needle-maintenance}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Remove the syringe from the autosampler or manual injection port
\item
  Inspect the needle for bends, burrs, or corrosion
\item
  Flush the syringe barrel with deionized water, then with dilute HCl
  (0.1 M), then with deionized water again
\item
  If the syringe plunger binds or the needle is damaged, replace the
  entire syringe assembly
\item
  Reinstall and run 3 deionized water blanks to verify proper function
\end{enumerate}

Moisture trap fills rapidly & High humidity; furnace tube leak & Drain

\section{Training Record}\label{training-record}

Before operating this instrument independently, personnel must:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Read this SOP in full and the relevant sections of the Shimadzu TOC-L,
  TNM-L, and SSM-5000A user manuals
\item
  Complete \href{SOP-L002-laboratory-safety.qmd}{SOP-L002 Laboratory
  Safety} and \href{SOP-L003-hazardous-materials-handling.qmd}{SOP-L003
  Hazardous Materials Handling} training
\item
  Observe a trained operator perform a complete session: cold start,
  calibration (carbon and nitrogen), sample analysis, data export, and
  transition to standby
\item
  Demonstrate competence preparing calibration standards (KHP and
  KNO\textsubscript{3}) per
  \href{SOP-L020-reagent-standard-preparation.qmd}{SOP-L020}
\item
  Perform a supervised analytical run with acceptable QC results (blanks
  below 0.1 mg/L, calibration verification recovery 90--110\%, replicate
  CV below 2\%)
\item
  Demonstrate entering and exiting standby and sleep modes
\item
  Be signed off by the instrument manager or PI
\end{enumerate}
