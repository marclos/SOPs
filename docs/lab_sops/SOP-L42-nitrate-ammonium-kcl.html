Soil Nitrate and Ammonium – KCl Extraction

Author
Affiliation

Aaron Rogers

EA Program, Pomona College

Published

2026-05-13

1. Purpose

This SOP describes the extraction of inorganic nitrogen (nitrate, NO3-, and ammonium, NH4+) from soil using 2 M potassium chloride (KCl). The filtered extracts are subsequently analyzed for NO3- and NH4+ concentrations using colorimetric or other analytical methods.

2. Scope

  • Applies to: Field-moist or air-dried soil samples collected per SOP-F40 Soil Sampling and Processing
  • Does not apply to: Total nitrogen analysis (requires digestion); dissolved nitrogen in water samples
  • Location: EA Program laboratory

3. Responsibilities

Role Responsibility
Lab PI Approve protocol, ensure training compliance
Trained Personnel Prepare extracts, record and report results
New Students Complete training before independent use

Required Training

Students using this SOP should be trained on:

  • SOP-F01 Field Safety
  • SOP-L02 Hazardous Materials Handling
  • SOP-L40 Soil Moisture

4. Safety and Hazards

WarningHazards
  • Chemical: 2 M KCl is a mild irritant; avoid contact with eyes and skin
  • Physical: Reciprocating shaker can pinch fingers if containers are not secured properly

Required PPE

5. Required Materials

Equipment

Item Notes
Reciprocating shaker For 2-hour extraction
Magnetic stirrer with stir bar For preparing KCl solution
Stir bar retriever Magnetic wand
Top-loading balance 0.1 g resolution
Graduated cylinder or volumetric flask 2 L capacity for KCl preparation
Repipet-type dispenser For dispensing KCl solution (optional)
Drying oven Set to 105 C (for moisture subsample)

Consumables

Item Notes
Whatman #42 filter paper Ashless, for filtering extracts
Filter funnels To fit filter paper
Test tubes or extraction bottles 50 mL capacity, with caps
Specimen containers or Nalgene bottles For collecting filtered extracts
Paper bags or aluminum weigh boats For moisture subsamples
Pipette For dispensing precise volumes

Reagents

Item Notes
Potassium chloride (KCl) ACS grade; 298.2 g per 2 L for 2 M solution
Distilled or deionized water For preparing KCl solution

6. Estimated Time

Task Time Required
KCl solution preparation 15 minutes active + 4 hours stirring
Moisture subsamples 5 minutes active + 24 hours drying
KCl extraction (per batch) 15 minutes setup + 2 hours shaking + 10 minutes settling
Filtering 20–30 minutes per batch

7. Procedure

7.1 Preparing 2 M KCl Solution

  1. Weigh out 298.2 g KCl into a 2 L volumetric flask
  2. Fill the flask approximately two-thirds full with distilled water
  3. Place the flask on a magnetic stirrer and add a magnetic stir bar
  4. Set the stirrer to low-medium speed and leave on for 4 hours until fully dissolved
  5. Use a stir bar retriever to remove the magnetic stir bar
  6. Fill the flask to the 2 L mark with distilled water and mix
TipTip

Prepare the KCl solution the day before extraction. It can be stored at room temperature in a capped container indefinitely.

7.2 Moisture Subsample

  1. Set aside 10 g of soil from each sample for moisture determination
  2. Place each 10 g subsample into a labeled aluminum weigh boat
  3. Dry at 105 C for 24 hours and record the dry weight
  4. Calculate gravimetric moisture content per SOP-L40 Soil Moisture

7.3 KCl Extraction

  1. Dispense 40 mL of 2 M KCl solution into labeled test tubes or extraction bottles
  2. Add 7 g of field-moist soil to each tube (the total volume should reach approximately 50 mL)
  3. Cap the tubes securely
  4. Place on the reciprocating shaker for 2 hours
  5. Remove from the shaker and allow soil particles to settle for approximately 10 minutes (do not exceed 1 hour)

7.4 Filtering

  1. Set up filter funnels with Whatman #42 filter paper over labeled specimen containers or clean test tubes
  2. Pour the shaken solution into the funnels; do not fill above the filter paper line
  3. Allow the solution to drain completely until only soil remains on the filter
  4. Discard the used filter paper and soil

7.5 Extract Storage

  1. Cap the filtered extract containers
  2. If analysis will be completed within a few days, store at 4 C
  3. If analysis will be delayed, freeze the extracts until analysis
Important

Extracts should be analyzed as soon as possible. Prolonged storage, even frozen, can alter NH4+ and NO3- concentrations due to microbial activity or chemical transformations.

8. Soil Texture Correction Factor

When converting extract concentrations to soil nutrient content, a soil texture correction factor may be applied:

Texture Moist Soil Factor Dry Soil Factor
Sand 2.3 2.6
Loam 2.0 2.5
Clay 1.7 2.2

9. Quality Control

  • Include a reagent blank (KCl solution only, no soil) in each extraction batch
  • Run extraction duplicates for at least 10% of samples
  • Use a laboratory reference soil with known inorganic N content when available
  • Record the KCl solution preparation date and batch number

10. Waste Disposal

Waste Type Disposal Method
KCl extracts Aqueous waste; drain with copious water if approved, or collect for EH&S
Used filter paper and soil Standard trash
Unused KCl solution Drain with water (non-hazardous)

12. Training Record

Before performing this procedure independently, personnel must:

  1. Read this SOP in full
  2. Complete SOP-L40 (Soil Moisture) training
  3. Demonstrate competence preparing KCl solution
  4. Perform the extraction and filtering procedure under supervision
  5. Be signed off by the PI or lab manager
