Total Carbon – Shimadzu TOC-L with SSM-5000A

Author
Affiliation

Marc Los Huertos

EA Program, Pomona College

Published

2026-05-13

1. Purpose

This SOP describes the operation of the Shimadzu TOC-L total organic carbon analyzer and the SSM-5000A Solid Sample Module for quantifying dissolved organic carbon (DOC) in soil extracts and total carbon (TC) or total organic carbon (TOC) in solid soil samples.

2. Scope

  • Applies to: K2SO4 extracts from the chloroform fumigation-extraction method (SOP-L04); water samples; and dried, ground solid soil samples
  • Does not apply to: Samples with extremely high salt concentrations that exceed the instrument’s tolerance (consult the instrument manual); sea water or marine sediments with high salt content
  • Location: EA Program analytical laboratory

3. Responsibilities

Role Responsibility
Lab PI Approve protocol, authorize instrument access
Instrument Manager Maintain instrument, replace catalyst and consumables
Trained Personnel Operate instrument, prepare standards and samples
New Students Complete training before independent use

4. Safety and Hazards

WarningHazards
  • Physical: Combustion furnace operates at 680 C (liquid mode) or up to 900 C (SSM mode); severe burn risk from ceramic boats and furnace components
  • Chemical: Carrier gas (high-purity air or oxygen); hydrochloric acid used for acidification and sparging of inorganic carbon
  • Compressed gas: High-pressure carrier gas cylinder

6. Procedure

6.1 Instrument Startup

  1. Verify carrier gas supply pressure (typically 200 kPa or per instrument manual)
  2. Turn on the TOC-L main unit and allow the furnace to reach operating temperature (680 C for liquid mode)
  3. Launch LabSolutions TOC software on the connected computer
  4. Run a system blank check using deionized water to verify baseline stability; the blank value should be below 0.1 mg/L C

6.2 Calibration

  1. Prepare a multi-point calibration curve using potassium hydrogen phthalate (KHP) dissolved in deionized water
  2. Typical calibration range: 0, 1, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100 mg/L C (adjust to expected sample range)
  3. For IC calibration (if measuring TC - IC = TOC), prepare sodium bicarbonate/carbonate standards
  4. Run calibration standards and verify that the correlation coefficient (r2) is at least 0.999
  5. Save the calibration curve in the software

6.3 Liquid Sample Analysis (DOC in Soil Extracts)

  1. Filter all liquid samples through 0.45 um syringe filters or pre-combusted glass fiber filters into clean autosampler vials
  2. For K2SO4 extracts, include 0.5 M K2SO4 blanks in the run
  3. Select the appropriate measurement mode in the software:
    • NPOC mode (recommended for most soil extracts): The instrument acidifies the sample with HCl, sparges with carrier gas to remove inorganic carbon as CO2, then combusts the remaining sample at 680 C. The evolved CO2 is measured by a non-dispersive infrared (NDIR) detector.
    • TC-IC mode (if both total and inorganic carbon are needed): TC is measured by direct combustion; IC is measured by acidification only. TOC = TC - IC.
  4. Load vials into the autosampler and start the run
  5. The instrument performs replicate injections (typically 3) and reports the mean and coefficient of variation
TipTip

If K2SO4 extracts have high salt content, use the instrument’s automatic dilution function to extend catalyst and combustion tube life. Dilution factors are applied automatically to the reported concentrations.

6.4 Solid Sample Analysis (SSM-5000A)

  1. Switch the instrument to SSM mode in the LabSolutions software
  2. Pre-combust ceramic boats at 900 C to remove any residual carbon
  3. Weigh 10–50 mg of dried, finely ground soil into a clean ceramic boat using an analytical balance (record mass to 0.01 mg)
  4. For TOC measurement (removing inorganic carbon): pre-treat the sample by adding a few drops of 2 M HCl to the boat, allow to react until effervescence stops, and dry at 105 C before analysis
  5. For TC measurement (no acid pretreatment): proceed directly
  6. Place the ceramic boat into the SSM sample port
  7. The SSM combusts the sample in an oxygen-rich environment at high temperature; the evolved CO2 is carried to the NDIR detector
  8. The software reports total carbon content in mg C or as a percentage of sample mass

6.5 Instrument Shutdown

  1. After the final sample, run 3–5 deionized water blanks to flush the system
  2. Review all data in the software for outliers or failed injections
  3. Export data and save the run file
  4. If the instrument will not be used for several days, turn off the furnace and carrier gas per the instrument manual

7. Quality Control

  • Run a calibration verification standard (mid-range) every 10–15 samples; acceptance criterion is within 10% of the known value
  • Include instrument blanks (deionized water) at the beginning, middle, and end of each run
  • Include method blanks (0.5 M K2SO4 for extract analyses) in each batch
  • Run sample duplicates at a rate of at least 1 per 10 samples
  • Monitor the coefficient of variation among replicate injections; re-run if CV exceeds 2% for liquid samples
  • Log catalyst replacement, combustion tube changes, and calibration dates in the instrument logbook

8. Maintenance

Task Frequency
System blank check Before each analytical run
Calibration verification Each run (mid-range check standard)
Combustion tube inspection Monthly or after approximately 500 injections
Catalyst replacement As needed (when blank values increase or sensitivity decreases)
Drain trap and humidifier check Weekly
Carrier gas cylinder replacement As needed (monitor pressure gauge)

9. Waste Disposal

Waste Type Disposal Method
Acidified liquid waste Neutralize and dispose per EH&S guidelines
Spent catalyst Collect for EH&S hazardous waste pickup
Used ceramic boats Clean and reuse, or dispose in standard trash
K2SO4 extract waste Aqueous waste; drain if approved, or collect

11. Training Record

Before operating this instrument independently, personnel must:

  1. Read this SOP and the relevant sections of the Shimadzu TOC-L and SSM-5000A user manuals
  2. Complete SOP-L02 Hazardous Materials Handling training
  3. Observe a trained operator perform startup, calibration, sample analysis, and shutdown
  4. Demonstrate competence with calibration standard preparation
  5. Perform a supervised analytical run with acceptable QC results
  6. Be signed off by the instrument manager or PI
