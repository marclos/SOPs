# Additions to SOP-F001 (Field Safety)

These blocks capture the tree-work safety content that was removed from
SOP-F607 and that is not yet covered in SOP-F001. Paste them into
SOP-F001-field-safety.qmd at the indicated locations, then bump the
revision and add a revision-history line.

Most of the tree-tagging hazards (poison oak, ticks, snakes, bees and
wasps, heat, lightning) are already covered in SOP-F001 Sections 6 and 8,
so they are not duplicated here. The genuinely missing items are overhead
tree hazards, hard-hat PPE, sharp hand-tool handling, and a consolidated
stop-work list.
